## Example

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\
4 & 5 & 6
\end{bmatrix}
\rightarrow^{Transpose}
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 4 \\
2 & 5 \\
3 & 6
\end{bmatrix}
$$
<center> Transposing a matrix is, in essence, rotating it. </center>
