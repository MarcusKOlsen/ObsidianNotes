## Definition
Vertex covering is a state in which all edges (their corresponding vertices) have been "hit" (has [[Maximum Flow]]).

The "size" of a vertex cover equals the amount of vertices in the graph.
## Bipartite
A bipartite graph has [[Maximum Bipartite Matching]] if it has the minimum vertex coverage. If every edge has been hit, a flow graph with a global capacity of 1 will by definition have max bipartite matching then.
